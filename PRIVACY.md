# Privacy Policy — Untangle

**Last updated: September 15, 2026**

## What the app does with your information

Untangle is a puzzle game that runs **entirely on your device**. The app
does not require registration or a user account to play.

## What information is stored on the device

The app stores the following locally on your device only (using the
device's local storage):

- **Game progress** — the furthest level you've reached, and (once Badge
  Challenge mode ships) your badge collection.
- **Preferences** — your sound-effects and haptic-feedback settings.

This information is never sent by us to any external server and is not
used by us for tracking. Deleting the app also deletes this information.
You can also clear it yourself at any time from Settings → Reset progress.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays interstitial ads through **Google AdMob** to help fund
its development and free distribution. The app currently ships with
Google's test ad unit IDs while in development; this section will be
updated once real ad units are in use. To serve ads, Google and its
advertising partners (AdTech partners) may collect and process
information such as:

- Device advertising identifier (Advertising ID / IDFA).
- IP address and general location (city/region level).
- Device type, operating system, and ad usage data (impressions, clicks).

This information is collected and processed by Google and its partners in
accordance with their own privacy policies, not directly by us. On
iPhone, access to the advertising identifier requires user consent
through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If permission isn't
granted, non-personalized ads are shown instead.

For more information on how Google uses this data:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Bug reports

The settings screen includes an optional form for reporting a bug. If
you choose to use it, the text you write — along with technical details
such as device type and app version — is sent to a third-party service
([Web3Forms](https://web3forms.com)) that forwards it to the app
developer's mailbox, solely to handle your report. Using this form is
entirely voluntary and isn't required to play the game.

## Information we do not collect ourselves

Beyond Google's advertising and any content you choose to submit through
the bug-report form, the app does not collect your name, precise
location, contacts, photos, or any other personally identifiable
information, and does not use any analytics services of its own.

## Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy changes in the future, the update will be
published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
